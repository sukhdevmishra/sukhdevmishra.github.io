PUBLICATION

PRISM-TB: machine learning for predicting drug-resistant tuberculosis ↗

A study published in the Indian Journal of Medical Research introduces the Protein-based Resistance In-silico Model (PRISM-TB), using protein sequence-derived features and machine learning to predict resistance to six anti-tuberculosis drugs. The Extra Trees model achieved an F1-score of 0.942 in internal evaluation. In external validation using the WHO mutation catalogue, the Random Forest model identified 93.9% of resistant mutations, although further validation is needed before clinical application.

Authors: Theja K.V., Ram Shankar Barai, Uma Devi Ranganathan, Swati Joshi, Achint Chaudhary, Agniva Das, and Sukhdev Mishra.

30 September 2026 · Indian Journal of Medical Research · DOI: 10.25259/IJMR_3532_2025
