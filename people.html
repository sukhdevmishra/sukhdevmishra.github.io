PROFILE

Research at the intersection of statistics, AI, and health

With nearly 20 years of research experience, I have worked across government-funded biomedical research, international clinical trials, and applied artificial intelligence and machine learning. My current work integrates statistical science and modern AI to study complex real-world health problems, develop predictive models, and support evidence-informed decisions.

My methodological interests include statistical learning, Bayesian modelling, causal inference, time-series forecasting, missing-data methods, generative models, deep learning, ensemble methods, and explainable AI.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

Areas of focus

Occupational & environmental health

Worker health, occupational exposures, air pollution, heat stress, respiratory health, and work-related musculoskeletal disorders.

AI and statistical learning

Generative modelling, machine learning, deep learning, ensemble/stacking methods, hyperparameter optimisation, and explainable prediction.

Biostatistics and causal inference

Bayesian modelling, time-series forecasting, missing-data methods, imputation, complex survey analysis, and doubly robust methods.

Biomedical and translational research

Clinical-trial design and analysis, biomarker prediction, microbiome research, diagnostic innovation, and systematic review/meta-analysis.

CURRENT RESEARCH

Selected funded projects

Generative AI for pneumoconiosis X-ray classification

Leveraging generative models and hyperparameter optimisation for accurate pneumoconiosis X-ray classification with statistical validation. ICMR-funded project; Principal Investigator.

Biomarkers of heavy-metal exposure

Identifying biomarkers of effects of heavy-metal exposure and evaluating them in differentially exposed populations across Indian states, including machine-learning approaches for exposure prediction. ICMR-funded project; Principal Investigator.

Other ongoing collaborative work listed in my CV includes studies of CC16 and neopterin for silicosis screening, lead and cadmium exposure and the oral microbiome, recombinant camelid nanobodies for cobra-venom neutralisation, and particulate-matter exposure-related pulmonary and cardiovascular dysfunction.
