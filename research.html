MISHRA RESEARCH LAB

Research

Our work connects population health, computational methods, and laboratory innovation.

RESEARCH PROGRAMME

Complex questions need connected thinking.

Our research spans multiple disciplines. The areas below describe the group's intended scope; specific projects, methods, and outputs should be updated as work progresses.

01

WORK · EXPOSURE · HEALTH

Occupational & Environmental Health

Population-based research on worker health, occupational exposures, respiratory health, musculoskeletal disorders, and environmental determinants of health.

  • Worker health and occupational risk
  • Environmental exposure assessment
  • Population-based health research
02

MODELLING · PREDICTION

AI & Machine Learning

Applying machine learning, time-series analysis, and ensemble modelling to public-health prediction and decision-support questions.

  • Predictive modelling and forecasting
  • Model evaluation and validation
  • Responsible, interpretable data science
03

INFERENCE · METHODS

Biostatistics & Evidence Synthesis

Developing and applying statistical methods for complex survey data, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, and reproducible research.

  • Survey-weighted analysis
  • Systematic reviews and meta-analysis
  • Research methods and reproducible workflows
04

MICROBES · DETECTION

Microbiology & Diagnostics

Exploring microbial systems and diagnostic concepts, including phage-inspired recognition approaches and nanoparticle-assisted detection.

  • Microbial recognition and detection
  • Phage-derived binding domains
  • Rapid diagnostic assay concepts
05

EXPOSURE · BIOMARKERS

Biomonitoring & Exposure Science

Integrating biomonitoring data and computational analysis to investigate exposure patterns and their potential health relevance.

  • Heavy-metal biomonitoring
  • Exposure data analysis
  • Machine learning for environmental health
06

INTEGRATION · TRANSLATION

Methods for Public Health

Combining study design, computational workflows, and evidence synthesis to strengthen the quality and usefulness of health research.

  • Analytical workflow development
  • Methodological evaluation
  • Translation of evidence into practice
